This page talks to your wallet through two calls only. The code contains no transaction building of any kind, and it makes no network requests after the page has loaded.

  • Phantom and Solflare are reached directly. MetaMask and other wallets are reached through the Wallet Standard, using its connect and sign message features only. MetaMask needs a Solana account, an Ethereum account cannot sign these receipts.
  • Read the message in your wallet before you approve it. It should start with MYELIN v1 and match the text shown on this page. If your wallet ever shows a transaction from this page, reject it.
  • Your wallet may still show a caution for a site it has not seen before. That is about the address of the page, not about what is being signed.
  • Progress is stored in this browser only. Clearing site data removes it, so keep a copy of your receipts.
  • The practice key is a throwaway key made in this browser so you can try the game without a wallet. It holds no funds and its receipts are marked as practice.

Sources and limits

Bones and brain parts are real 3D data from BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International, with geometry simplified by the open source Human Atlas project.

Nerve routes are schematic. Each nerve is a curve drawn along the real bones from standard anatomy, accurate to roughly a centimetre or two. Good for learning routes, not for clinical use.

  • Tiers use one speed per nerve, a typical figure for its fastest fibres. Real nerves carry a mix of fast and slow fibres and values vary between people.
  • The nine bonus bones (six ear ossicles, two inferior nasal conchae, the coccyx) are missing from the scan. The versions here are simple hand made stand ins and are labelled as reconstructed.
  • A receipt proves which wallet signed which message. It does not prove when, and it cannot be transferred to another wallet.

Educational model, not a diagnostic tool.
